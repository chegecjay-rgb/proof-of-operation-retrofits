# CERTIFICATION ARTIFACT CLASSIFICATION

## Purpose

Classify certification artifacts for publication readiness.

## Publication Classification

The following categories are considered publication-facing artifacts:

- repository verification reports
- publication verification matrices
- reviewer journey reports
- publication risk registers
- publication path validation reports
- ecosystem completion matrices
- publication integration reports
- freeze readiness evidence
- publication readiness evidence

These artifacts may remain within the publication surface.

## Internal Classification

The following categories are considered internal engineering artifacts:

- temporary markers
- operational checkpoints
- recovery worksheets
- extraction workplans
- execution logs
- intermediate evidence collections

These artifacts are not required for reviewer-facing publication surfaces.

## Determination

Certification artifacts are classified.

Reviewer-facing publication surface may selectively expose publication-classified artifacts while excluding internal engineering records.
